Why Fernhill

Every listing earns its place here.

We turn away more homes than we list. Tap a principle to see what it means in practice.

We inspect the bones before the finish. Structural timber, foundation health and insulation are checked before a home ever gets photographed — long before it earns a listing page.

Reclaimed oak, local stone, low-VOC finishes. Where we can trace where a material came from, we tell you — in the listing, not in small print.

Every listing includes a short walk-through of what's nearby on foot — the baker, the allotments, the school run — written by someone who actually walked it.

No handoffs between viewing and completion. The person who shows you the home is the person who carries the paperwork home.
